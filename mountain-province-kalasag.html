Traditional Shield

Tactical Purpose: Used as a defensive shield against enemy weapons such as spears and axes. Its pointed prongs could also help trip, pin, or restrain opponents during close combat.

Materials Used: Carved from lightweight native hardwoods such as dapdap, polay, or sablang. The shield is reinforced with woven rattan (uway) bands and coated with dark resin for added strength and protection.
