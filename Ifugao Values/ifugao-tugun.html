Cultural and Moral Significance: Reflects the importance of respecting ancestral teachings and fulfilling responsibilities inherited from previous generations. It encourages individuals to uphold traditional values, follow moral guidance, and maintain cultural practices within their communities.

Representation and Meaning: Represents the passing down of wisdom, knowledge, and moral duties from elders to younger generations. It emphasizes respect for ancestral guidance, the responsibility to practice inherited teachings, and the importance of preserving these values for future generations.
