Cultural and Ritual Purpose: Used by the Ibaloi and Kankanaey to provide rhythm for traditional dances such as the tayaw and bendiyan. It is also played during harvest celebrations, healing ceremonies, weddings, and other community gatherings.

Materials Used:: Traditionally made from a hollowed-out log or pipe shaped into a long, narrow drum body. An animal-skin drumhead, often made from deer or pig skin, is stretched over the wider opening and secured with rattan strips or strong lacings.
