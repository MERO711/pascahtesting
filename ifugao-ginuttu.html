Ginuttu

Traditional Shell Belt

Purpose: Traditionally worn by wealthy elite men known as kadangyan as a ceremonial belt and sword belt. It is displayed during important celebrations, marriage rituals, funeral rites, and ceremonies recognizing social rank.

Materials Used: Made from hand-carved, disc-shaped shell pieces, often sourced from giant clams or mother-of-pearl. The discs are strung together using rattan, cotton thread, or bark thread and completed with a central shell or wooden buckle called an upod.
