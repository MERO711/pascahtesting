Bulul

Traditional Sculpture

Agricultural and Ritual Purpose: Placed inside rice granaries (alang) and homes to protect stored rice, encourage good harvests, and guard against pests or theft. It is also used in sacred rituals led by traditional priests (mumbaki).

Materials Used: Carved mainly from hardwoods such as narra, ipil, or tree fern (gulgul). The figures are shaped into simple human forms and may darken over time from smoke and ritual offerings.
