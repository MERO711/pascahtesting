Cultural and Moral Significance: Reflects the importance of remaining hopeful during difficult situations, such as poor harvests, personal struggles, or loss. It encourages individuals to acknowledge their problems while maintaining a positive outlook toward the future.

Representation and Meaning: Represents the Kankanaey attitude of acknowledging grief, hardships, and challenges while maintaining hope for better outcomes. It emphasizes resilience, perseverance, and the belief that difficulties can be overcome with patience and determination.
