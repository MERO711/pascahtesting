Sinawit

Traditional Head Axe

Purpose: A traditional head axe historically associated with Cordilleran warriors. It was used as a weapon and is connected to traditional warrior practices and historical conflicts.

Materials Used: Features a curved blade made from forged steel or iron, sometimes using recycled metal. The blade has a distinctive rear spike and is attached to a hardwood handle, with metal bands reinforcing the connection.
