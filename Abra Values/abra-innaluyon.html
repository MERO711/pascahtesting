Traditional Practice of Mutual Help and Cooperation

Cultural and Moral Significance: Reflects the importance of unity, generosity, and cooperation within the community. It encourages individuals to assist one another, share responsibilities, and strengthen relationships through mutual support and reciprocal help.

Representation and Meaning: Represents the tradition of working together to accomplish shared goals and overcome challenges. It emphasizes community solidarity, collective responsibility, and the understanding that helping others strengthens relationships and encourages mutual assistance in the future.
