Atang

Traditional Food-Offering Ritual

Agricultural and Ritual Purpose: Laid out on household altars, grave tombs, or outdoor clearings to honor the souls of the deceased (umalagad), remember relatives during Undas, or seek healing from illnesses caused by unseen spirits. It is also used in localized rituals led by family elders or traditional spiritual practitioners.

Ritual Elements and Offerings: Composed mainly of local delicacies such as sticky rice cakes (suman or patopat), sliced boiled eggs, and pork. These food elements are paired with traditional items like native rice wine (tapuy), rolled tobacco leaves, or betel nut chews (mama), arranged on native woven trays or banana leaves.
