Tinali

Traditional Beaded Arm Ornaments

Aesthetic Purpose: Traditionally worn by women in pairs as tightly wrapped, layered armbands extending from the wrists to the forearms and sometimes reaching the upper arms. They are commonly worn during formal and ceremonial occasions.

Materials Used: Made from hundreds of colorful antique glass seed beads (batek) arranged into intricate patterns of red, yellow, orange, white, black, and blue. The beads are individually tied by hand and secured with threads, cloth sleeves, or small buttons.
