Aliwa

Traditional Curved-Blade Tool

Everyday and Combat Purpose: Used for clearing thick vegetation, chopping firewood, and harvesting crops. Historically, it also served as a close-combat weapon, with its curved blade designed for effective swinging and striking.

Materials Used: Features a forged steel blade with a broad, curved edge and a distinctive backward-pointing spur. Its handle is made from hardwood or carabao horn and reinforced with woven rattan (uway) for a secure grip.
