Purpose: Used by the Tinguian (Itneg) people to carry seeds, harvested crops, tools, and other supplies across rugged mountain trails. Its sturdy structure helps protect the contents during transport.

Materials Used: Traditionally made from processed forest materials, with a rigid, box-like shape and a fitted lid or top flap. Rattan forms the main structure, while woven nito vines or split bamboo reinforce the edges and joints. It may also include woven shoulder straps for carrying.
