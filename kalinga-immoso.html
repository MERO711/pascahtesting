Immoso

Traditional Ceramic Jars

Everyday Purpose: Used for collecting and storing water, fermenting and aging traditional rice or sugarcane wine (basi), and serving beverages during household and community gatherings.

Materials Used: Made from local earthenware clay using hand-modeling, coiling, and paddle-and-anvil techniques. The jars are polished with a smooth stone, decorated with geometric patterns, pit-fired, and coated with tree resin (dikay) for durability and water resistance.
