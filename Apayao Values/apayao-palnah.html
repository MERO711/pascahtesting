Traditional Intertribal Peace Pact and Conflict Resolution System

Cultural and Moral Significance: Reflects the importance of maintaining peace, fairness, and mutual respect among communities. It emphasizes the role of elders in resolving disputes, encouraging understanding, and promoting cooperation to prevent further conflicts.

Representation and Meaning: Represents the Isnag tradition of settling disagreements through peaceful discussions, mediation, and mutual agreements. It emphasizes reconciliation, respect for traditional laws, and the importance of restoring harmony and strengthening relationships between communities.
