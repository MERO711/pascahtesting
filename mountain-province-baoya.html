Baoya

Traditional Necklace

Purpose: Traditionally worn by elite Bontok men, warriors, and priests during major celebrations, traditional dances, and agricultural ceremonies such as the begnas. It is also associated with historical warrior traditions and may be passed down as a family heirloom.

Materials Used: Made from wild boar tusks or polished mother-of-pearl shaped like teeth. These pieces are arranged and secured to a sturdy woven band made from rattan and runo reeds.
