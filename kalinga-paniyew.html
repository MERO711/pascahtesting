Moral and Cultural Purpose: Encourages individuals to avoid wrongdoing, such as stealing, harming others, or disrespecting cultural prohibitions. It promotes self-discipline, respect, and responsible behavior.

Cultural Significance: Reflects the belief that violating certain moral or cultural prohibitions may bring misfortune or harmful consequences. It serves as a traditional guide for maintaining harmony and respect within the community.
